Boltz / GNINA comparison

Experiment goal and settings

Baseline: C25, the starting 25-carbon chain. Each Δ is molecule minus C25 in the same scorer; GNINA pairs the same available sampling seeds. Negative Vina and native affinity changes, and positive CNN changes, are favorable predictions. JQ1 is a separate reference. These are selected molecules, not a full optimization trajectory.

Boltz quantitative affinity is log10(IC50 in μM) and is intended for comparisons among active compounds. C25 and the generated molecules are unvalidated, so its Δ is a diagnostic model-output change. The binder output is a separate head. Boltz output definitions.

Filled plot markers preserve coordinate stereo; hollow orange markers have a mismatch or an incomplete repeat set. Dashed lines mark C25. GNINA: mean [range] of the first written pose across three seeds. Native Boltz: one seed. A stereo mismatch means the actual coordinates represent a different isomer; its raw score remains visible but is excluded from a matched-compound claim. Pocket placement and basic geometry are separate checks.

Inspect the actual scored pose

Drag to rotate; scroll to zoom. Colored sticks: scored ligand. Purple outline: deposited JQ1 reference. Gray dots: pocket atoms; gray line: protein backbone. Native complexes are rigidly aligned by protein Cα atoms to the reference for display; this does not alter scores or distances. The native pose is the exact sample passed to both affinity heads.
