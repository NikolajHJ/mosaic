All six selected studies completed their declared budgets. A completed computation is not a chemical-validity or binding certificate. The first study uses a different protein, atom count and contact definition. Later chemistry studies also changed optimization parameterization.


What these results establish
  • The fixed-atom relaxed graph can receive validated gradients from both frozen model integrations for the included starts.
  • Rule-based chemistry guidance can produce a connected valid endpoint from random initialization, while its zero signal on valid graphs can leave the validity boundary unstable.
  • Boltz-2 introduces typed-bond sensitivity and an affinity surrogate, with a declared fixed-pose partial-gradient scope.
What remains open
  • No generated output has demonstrated experimental BRD4 binding, selectivity or synthesis feasibility.
  • The separate Boltz random-start diagnostic finished, but reproduced the original failed bond finite-difference gate. Its long optimization was not submitted.
  • Continuous chemistry guidance, a gentler ramp and LR 0.01 remain untested proposals. The included newest runs used LR 0.03.
Repair targets and weights are held fixed within each update’s trials, then refreshed. Recorded within-update improvements do not imply one globally decreasing combined objective. Binder probability is separate from quantitative affinity.
