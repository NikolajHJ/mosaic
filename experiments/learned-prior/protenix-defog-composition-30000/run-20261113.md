## 15 November progress and preliminary results

The corrected reference reached normal updates and the controller automatically
submitted all remaining 387 bundles on 13 November, finishing at 15:42 CET.
All 396 corrected bundles have accepted receipts.

At 21:21 CET on 15 November, 1,294/1,395 runs had completed all 500 updates,
96 had not started, and five had stopped early. The remaining 26 queued bundles
were 24 A100 and two A10; none was running at that snapshot. All five partial
runs stopped on the valence solver's KKT tolerance, with maximum reported excess
between 3.7e-6 and 4.7e-5. Their partial trajectories and frozen checks are retained.
They are distinct from the original PDB packaging failures.

Constant DeFoG weight 0.5, teacher time 0.8, connected projection and no cooling
is promising on the requested best-valid criterion. Its nine completed
seed/start runs all found a new valid connected molecule; four finished valid.
One matched control stopped at update 226, so full-budget comparisons use only
the eight completed pairs across the three seeds:

These are preliminary comparisons after exploring 155 settings, not a held-out
confirmation or an affinity measurement. Seeds 71 and 101 remain untested.

Most configurations still fragment late. The pooled counts describe this
campaign and do not estimate a causal teacher benefit.
