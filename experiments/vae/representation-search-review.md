# Which molecular representation should drive adaptive search?

17 November 2026. Research review of DBMol, its adaptive-restart worktree, and Mosaic’s research and main branches. The question is whether a representation yields **distinct, valid molecular edits that retain a target-score improvement after fresh scoring**. Exact reconstruction, unconditional generation, local editability and target-score transfer each measure a different part of that goal.

## Recommendation

Keep epoch 45 frozen as the search reference. It already powers the best evidenced adaptive result. Keep epoch 46 as the training parent for short comparisons. The saved mid-epoch-47 checkpoint has a specific random-generation failure at the 50-slot limit. I would hold off on further full-corpus training decisions until a loss change corrects that behavior. First test variable-size proposals inside the successful adaptive controller, and include a direct-graph arm to measure the contribution of the VAE. No training recipe or queue was changed for this review.

## What the training objective rewards

- **Epoch 46 had explicit protection against drift.** Its 1:1 real/prior recipe retained a frozen occupancy reference, valid targets from a frozen model at corresponding prior vectors, real and prior repair, and composition guidance. Epoch 47 uses reconstruction and KL on real molecules, plus an atom-preserving repair target on 16 fresh priors per 240 real molecules. The prior term is 0.375 times its own batch mean. The ratio changes sampling variance and the number of priors per update. It does not divide that coefficient by another 15. One pass through 60M is 250,000 optimizer updates, compared with about 28,678 for epoch 46. [Epoch-46 recipe](combined-training.md) · [Current recipe](epoch46-pubchem60m-prior15-v100-benchmark.json) · [Training branches](../../src/latentmol/training/combined_continuation.py)
- **The current teacher can fix bonds but cannot teach a different number of atoms.** A successful repair retains the student’s exact atom labels, occupancy, and charges. Full-target cross-entropy rewards these labels along with the corrected bonds. If the decoder already filled 50 slots, a successful repair supplies 50 occupied targets. Already-valid and unsuccessfully repaired rows contribute zero to this term. At 25 occupied atoms the accepted graph’s 375 decisions comprise 50 atom slots, 25 charges and 300 atom pairs. At 50 there are 1,325 decisions. A falling average can coexist with an incorrect size distribution and a few critical wrong bonds. This is a demonstrated mismatch between objective and intended behavior. Which change initiated the drift remains open because corpus, references, losses, ratio and number of updates changed together. [Proposal rules](../../src/latentmol/guidance/repair_proposals.py) · [Prior update](../../src/latentmol/training/prior_repair.py) · [Repair NLL](../../src/latentmol/atom_preserving_chemistry.py)
- **There is historical evidence for a persistent target.** On two earlier replayed updates the reconstruction-gradient norm on the decoder was about 27–28 times the repair-gradient norm. Lower NO_BOND weighting alone failed in a subsequent pilot. In another 2,500-update check, a frozen valid-reference recipe improved raw argmax validity from 641 to 665 out of 1,024 and categorical validity from 2,091 to 2,323 out of 4,096. These studies used different training states. They support retesting a reference on the current branch. They do not identify a universal weight. ## Is the space too sharp?

**Near encoded molecules, yes for ordinary small updates.** On six illustrative encodings at temperature 1, the median per-molecule fraction with a winning probability above 0.99 was 100% for occupied atom types and for present bonds. On six Gaussian starts the corresponding figures were about 77% and 91%. At epoch 45, four perturbations per anchor around 1,000 held-out encodings produced zero valid changed identities at each of four tested scales. Around 1,000 prior draws, 856 of 4,000 trials produced a changed valid identity at the smallest scale. These perturbations use different scaling conventions; their largest mean L2 displacements were 2.91 and 3.20, respectively. On 10,000 untouched-by-repair prior draws, epoch 45 yielded 7,541 distinct valid argmax molecules and 6,504 distinct scaffolds. High confidence in each prediction can coexist with considerable diversity across the space. [Probability measurements](../../outputs/latent-landscape/protenix-latent-landscape-20261104a/summary.json) · [Matched 10k evaluation and perturbations](checkpoint-comparison.md) · [Per-checkpoint metrics](../../outputs/hpc-results/vae-epoch43-45-comparison-20261103-a02/completed-summary.json)

The gradient evidence points to plateaus and a choice of step length. On five native Protenix saved/prior centers, neighboring contact gradients had median cosine 0.995 at radius 0.1 and temperature 1. On four separately tested held-out encodings, normalized descent reached a valid fixed-slot edit in 4/4 versus 0/8 random rays; only one was reached by radius 1. Warming to 1.5 increased encoded gradient norms without changing the hard identity of any of three encoded starts in that short run. Temperature changes probabilities and derivatives; at a fixed latent it preserves argmax rankings. Larger steps can also lose local directional accuracy. Start-specific radii and inexpensive trial decodes are an immediate intervention. [Landscape](../latent-design/protenix-vae/landscape.md) · [Boundary, temperature and bounded step results](../latent-design/protenix-vae/editability.md)

Generic interpolation has already had a fairer test than mixed targets alone. Mixed atom/bond targets yielded only 42 valid argmax midpoints out of 128 even when matched exactly. Supervision with actual valid intermediate molecules still took sampled-prior validity from 63.27% in the control to 52.51% in the treatment. The slot-aligned, gradient-capped follow-up protected most prior performance and gave 59/64 valid midpoints in both arms. Canonical input ordering did help atom-shuffle robustness. It cannot align the same atom across different analogues by itself. I would keep interpolation as an evaluation and put training effort into local edits that the optimizer actually proposes. These scores have different definitions. A freshly reimported hard-graph Protenix contact score is evidence about a particular model prediction. A Boltz fixed-pose composite, a native affinity prediction, a synthetic accessibility heuristic and experimental binding are separate endpoints. In particular, two long restart archives contained highly caged molecules despite their favorable contact scores. The best molecule in an archive and the last point of a trajectory answer different questions. The three-parent direct-edit pilot is particularly relevant to the choice of representation. With eight fresh attempts per parent per arm, direct graph edits and latent restarts had essentially the same mean best loss (2.45124 and 2.45228). Latent search found the best individual molecule and four candidates below 2.40 versus one. Three parents are insufficient for choosing a winner, but this is a much more informative control than comparing a VAE solely against its own earlier checkpoints. ## The comparison I would run next

1. **Separate the VAE’s contribution from the controller.** Start with the same valid 25-atom founders, same pocket, same fresh scorer and same adaptive parent archive. Compare epoch-45 latent updates with direct relaxed-graph updates. For each founder verify matching hard identity under both representations. Keep separate strata for known-molecule encodings, validated prior founders and restarts. Compare at common fresh-call prefixes as well as common relaxed-call allowances. Count failed conformers, duplicates, decoder proposals and elapsed time. Report the number of distinct valid fresh improvements and top-three mean per seed, alongside scaffold diversity and chemistry flags. Confirm finalists on independent geometry seeds withheld from search and at least a second pocket before generalizing.
2. **Give the same controller one variable-size branch per parent.** Keep ordinary latent updates inside a child. At a branch boundary try the frozen decoder’s native-scored addition or deletion proposal alongside a cost-matched random latent size proposal. Use the existing raw-occupancy implementation. Rebuild native inputs at the proposed count and admit a child after its unmasked hard graph is valid and scored. Store latent, mask, raw identity, scored identity and atom count together. Some fixed-25 parents change chemistry when their mask is removed; treat that as a new candidate with its own baseline or retain the parent only in the fixed-size arm. Track count-change opportunities, realized additions/removals, and score improvements within starting-size bins. A count change alone is not the success criterion. [Adaptive controller](C:/Users/nikol/.codex/worktrees/protenix-restart-search/DBMol_exploratory/src/latentmol/design/protenix_search.py) · [Native count proposals](../../src/latentmol/protenix_occupancy/raw_occupancy.py) · [Observed mask-induced identity loss](C:/Users/nikol/.codex/worktrees/protenix-restart-search/DBMol_exploratory/experiments/latent-design/protenix-vae/restart-synthesis-next.md)
3. **Test the training mechanism from epoch 46 at small cost.** Replay the first 15:1 updates on the same shuffled data, seeds, and optimizer state. Compare the current loss with one added population-level size constraint on the random branch. For example, average the predicted count distributions across a batch or accumulated batches and match an explicitly chosen reference histogram. Per-slot occupancy is `1 − p(NO_ATOM)`; under the implemented sampling law its sum is the expected count, and the distribution of the sum of Bernoulli occupancies gives a differentiable count histogram. Track both the predicted distribution and observed raw argmax counts. Choose the training histogram if broad size coverage is the goal. It need not pin individual molecules to the parent’s 22-atom median. As a separate control, restore the earlier frozen occupancy reference, and separately the frozen valid-target term if size stabilizes but chemistry still deteriorates. Measure prior validity and diversity on the same vectors after 0, 50, 200 and 1,000 updates; extend through the point where the matched control deteriorates. Record prior size histogram, atom/bond errors, reconstruction by size/source and per-loss gradient norms on the occupancy parameters. A training-population prior need not be an individual design-time size penalty.
4. **Test capacity after fixing the comparison.** The current model has **3,941,860 parameters**, 2,173,216 encoder and 1,768,644 decoder. Four triangle blocks account for only 33,600. A concrete compact model retains the 256-D latent and four refinement rounds while halving atom/pair/triangle widths to 48/24/12 and using a 256-wide pooled encoder. It has **1,700,836 parameters**. Compare both from comparable initialization and exposure to the same data, loss, optimizer updates and evaluation starts. A short pilot identifies learning curves; allowing both models enough training avoids mistaking slower convergence for inadequate capacity. A second ablation can vary the latent dimension separately. A 64-D search subspace inside the frozen 256-D model is a cheap way to test whether the optimizer benefits from fewer directions before retraining either network. ## Alternatives if the current representation loses that comparison

- **A local edit embedding.** Encode a parent graph plus a small continuous vector that selects a coordinated local edit. Decode an add, remove, substitution or attachment with its bonds as a unit. The parent supplies identity and the search retains its continuous proposal variable. This directly trains the operation required at an adaptive restart. A discrete edit head needs candidate scoring or a declared relaxation for its gradient. [Graph-to-graph molecular optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.01070)
- **A motif or junction-tree decoder.** Assembling compatible fragments can reduce invalid intermediate proposals and gives natural size changes. Its fragment vocabulary and discrete assembly decisions limit the convenience of the current end-to-end relaxed scorer gradient. I would test it as a competing proposal generator behind the same archive first. [Junction Tree VAE](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/jin18a.html)
- **Local latent search with a trust region.** Adjust the radius around each freshly scored parent based on whether it produces distinct, valid, improving graphs. This uses the existing decoder and can be evaluated without a learned scorer. LOL-BO provides a published example of local latent optimization for structured inputs, with its own learned surrogate and benchmarks. A gradient-free graph-edit comparator also measures the cost-benefit of learning a representation at all. [Local latent optimization](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/ded98d28f82342a39f371c013dfb3058-Abstract-Conference.html) · [Graph-based search baseline](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2019/sc/c8sc05372c)

My decision rule is whether the frozen latent adds diverse, freshly verified improvements per scorer call above the same controller’s direct-graph or random proposals. If it does, even a sharp representation can be useful. If it loses consistently on new starts and pockets, a compact local-edit model is a more informative next investment than another long reconstruction run.
