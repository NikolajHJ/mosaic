## Search and scoring rules

1. **Choose three replayable near-miss founders.** Start from connected hard molecules with audited synthetic accessibility (SA) ≤5 and a fresh BRD4 contact loss in (2.3, 2.4]. Rank by lowest fresh loss and greedily retain a diverse panel with pairwise Morgan radius-2 Tanimoto ≤0.60. The runner checks that the saved latent reproduces the recorded hard graph on the original occupied slots. This preset requires a qualifying archive. For a new archive, screen previously unscored hard molecules first and save replayable near-misses. [Starting-panel comparison](restart-synthesis-founders.md) · [Archive screen](restart-synthesis-archive-screen.md)
2. **Optimize three children for 500 updates per wave, over three waves.** Use LR 60, step norm cap 0.25, chemistry and valence weights 1, and a weak SynFrag gradient capped at 0.05 of the reference gradient norm. Perturb each starting latent in wave one. Wave two protects the best freshly scored parent, chooses the others using binding/SA Pareto ranking and structural diversity, then perturbs the chosen parents. Use a 100-update same-branch spacing rule and parent similarity ceiling 0.85 during this selection. The runner records any relaxed constraints required to fill the panel. Wave three continues each preceding branch using its recorded terminal or recovery state. Update noise is zero throughout. [Runnable preset](restart-synthesis-standard.json)
3. **Score valid intermediates as well as endpoints.** Each branch writes per-update hard identity, validity and relaxed loss to `metrics.jsonl`, plus latents and discrete labels in `raw-blocks/`. Among its replayable, previously unscored hard identities, prioritize audited SA ≤5 by lowest relaxed loss with pairwise Morgan similarity ≤0.65. Fill unused slots from the (5, 6] SA interval under the same diversity rule. Unfilled slots stay unused. The ≤5 ranking is supported by the archive screen, while the ≤6 fallback is a prospective budget choice. Allow three fresh founder scores and up to six new scores per child, for **4,500 optimizer updates and up to 57 discovery evaluations**. Retain successful fresh scores, source branches, and parent states in the wave outputs. [Archive-screen outcome](restart-synthesis-archive-screen.md)
4. **Measure primary hits on the same hard argmax graph.** Require audited pre-smoothing SA ≤5 and fresh Protenix loss ≤2.3. Report SA ≤6 separately. Count unique identities and fingerprint-diverse hits, separately showing yields after founder and recorded historical exclusions. Report actual attempts and successes alongside allowances. Save raw RDKit SA as a companion value. The best relaxed loss is a ranking signal during the search. [Hard SA implementation](../../../src/latentmol/evaluation/synthesis_accessibility.py)
5. **Confirm finalists after the search.** Freeze up to three strict hits per arm by discovery loss, excluding founders and known identities in the input panel. Fresh-score each distinct finalist once on each of geometry seeds 2203 and 3209. Save individual scores, mean and range in `confirmation/result.json`; a confirmed hit passes 2.3 on both seeds. An incomplete pair stays unconfirmed. These calls have a separate allowance of six per arm and never feed back into parent selection. For claims about a synthesis route, pass confirmed identities to the recorded stock-and-budget route planner. A low SA score alone did not predict a completed route in our 24-molecule pilot. [Route protocol and controls](restart-synthesis-route.md)
