# BRD4 search with the epoch-48 VAE

Can two independent founder panels from the epoch-48 VAE produce high-scoring, valid BRD4 candidates? The search optimizes only the latent vector and ranks distinct valid molecules by their freshly predicted Protenix pocket-contact loss. The full frozen encoder and decoder are restored through the repository loader. Both jobs pin checkpoint update 623116 and SHA-256 `504fccee8954f684bace24c84e3b04af239260927531248b3ce5f974725d5df8`. [Run configuration](restart-brd4-e48.json)

Each run uses 25 occupied slots, LR 60, a step cap of 0.5, constant chemistry and valence weights of 1 and zero update noise. The first wave optimizes four founders screened from 64 epoch-48 prior draws. Each of five further waves takes up to four eligible parents and two children per parent, with two exact continuations per wave. Other children use a screened latent perturbation. Each branch receives 500 updates. The cap per run is 22,000 updates and 264 fresh scores. Actual consumption depends on eligible parents and distinct valid candidates. The search saves a score-ranked top 20 and a separate fingerprint-diverse panel. Founders can enter the ranking, so use each first-wave `bootstrap-screen.json` to identify which candidates are new.

## Focused search from M02

M02 was a prior BRD4 hit with audited SA 3.15. It scored 2.2523 on the discovery geometry and 2.2569 and 2.2533 on two held-out ligand geometries. The epoch-48 encoder reconstructed its canonical molecule from the encoder mean. A fresh Protenix score at launch establishes the current baseline. The search then runs six waves with one parent and three children per wave, 500 updates per child and the same LR 60, 0.5 cap and chemistry settings as the broad searches. One slot requests an exact continuation and two slots use screened latent perturbations. If an exact start was already used, the runner attempts to replace it. The next parent has the lowest fresh discovery loss among replayable molecules. [Epoch-48 source and configuration](restart-brd4-e48-m02.json)

The cap is 9,000 updates, 108 fresh discovery scores, one founder score and up to eight confirmation scores. The ranked novel panel excludes the starting molecule and records each candidate’s discovery score, change from the founder and audited SA. The three best novel molecules and the founder are rescored on geometry seeds 2203 and 3209 after selection. A two-wave, 12-update CPU smoke replayed M02 from the exact checkpoint, propagated a differentiable surrogate score through the frozen decoder, and exercised the empty novel panel and held-out scoring. A separate CPU fixture checked nonempty ranking and all eight held-out score slots. Native contact predictions come from the allocated run.

## Paired Protenix and Nesso-1 search from M02

Does adding a Nesso-1 affinity gradient help the epoch-48 VAE find BRD4 molecules with better predicted affinity and pocket contact than Protenix alone? The [combined preset](restart-brd4-e48-m02-nesso.json) starts from the same encoded M02, seed 9312401, occupancy, optimizer and restart settings as control job 30359672. Each arm gets six waves of three 500-update children, LR 60, a 0.5 step cap, chemistry and valence weights of 1, and zero update noise. The search budget is 9,000 updates, up to 108 fresh discovery contact scores, one founder score and up to eight held-out contact evaluations.

The two frozen models receive the same 25 occupied slots and their relaxed atom, charge and bond probabilities. Nesso uses the founder conformer and its protein crop for the gradient. The search probes both gradients with respect to the epoch-48 latent vector at M02 and at two nearby points, then fixes the weights for the run. The target is equal median contributions and a median combined norm matching Protenix alone, before the chemistry terms and clipping. Parent selection in both arms uses fresh Protenix contact. At readout, each successfully contact-scored distinct molecule and M02 get an independently prepared native Nesso affinity and binder probability estimate. See the [latent calibration and blend](../../../src/latentmol/design/protenix_nesso_blend.py) and [matched readout](../direct-jump/compare_e48_nesso.py).

## Wider and deeper search with 100-update branches

Does spreading the same compute over shorter branches and more waves improve the yield and scores of valid BRD4 molecules? The two repeats use the same epoch-48 checkpoint, Protenix contact objective, four screened starts per seed, LR 60, 25 occupied atoms, step cap 0.5, chemistry and valence weights of 1, and no update noise. The first wave spends 100 updates per founder. Each of 12 later waves allows up to six eligible parents and three children per parent, with 100 updates per child. The frontier still enforces the original spacing and diversity rules, so the number of eligible parents can limit realized width, especially in the first restart wave.

Each arm caps at 220 branches, 22,000 updates and 264 fresh scores, the same update and scoring budgets as the 500-update controls. The founder wave allows 24 scores and each restart wave allows 20. When a wave has more eligible candidates than scoring slots, it gives each branch a first score before taking additional picks. Compare best fresh loss, top-20 scores and distinct valid yield at shared actual update and fresh-score counts. This experiment changes branch length, width and depth together.
