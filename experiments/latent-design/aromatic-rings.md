# Does the search generate aromatic rings?

19 November follow-up. In a new direct-graph proposal pilot without a VAE, localized bond matching produced an aromatic ring in 75/768 attempts from six initially nonaromatic real validation molecules. Sixteen outcomes included a new ring-closing edge. After a stricter check on bond types and SMILES, 65 outcomes remained versus zero for matched independent edits. [Mosaic experiment and scorer checks](../../../mosaic/experiments/brd4-jq1/protenix-random-chem/aromatic-formation/README.md)

18 November 2026. **Yes for DBMol's VAE and adaptive search, but aromatic rings are rare in the generated molecules and absent from the highest-ranked BRD4 candidates.** An adaptive trajectory went from a valid molecule with no rings to a valid molecule containing a pyrimidine, a six-membered aromatic ring with two nitrogens. The direct Mosaic campaign examined here produced no five- or six-membered aromatic ring in 13,010 distinct valid molecules. [Adaptive trajectories][matched] · [Direct-graph audit][mosaic]

![Two saved hard molecules from the same adaptive search branch, with the pyrimidine ring highlighted in the later molecule](../../outputs/runs/aromaticity-20261118/aromatic-trajectory.png)

The figure shows seed C, cycle 4, parent 07, at steps 53 and 182. Each graph has 25 heavy atoms. These are two readouts of the optimization trajectory, 129 updates apart. In a second branch, a valid nonaromatic step-0 graph yielded a benzene-containing molecule at step 28 that received a fresh Protenix loss of 2.265. Across all three seeds, **31/259** adaptive branches with valid nonaromatic starts produced an aromatic molecule later, and **12/259** had one freshly scored. Only four adaptive branches started valid and aromatic. Two ended aromatic, so this experiment gives little precision on retention from aromatic starts. ## How often do they appear?

**Raw VAE bond labels, 20 November.** Both common SMILES spellings of benzene encode to six AROMATIC training labels. A manually supplied alternating SINGLE/DOUBLE ring also decodes to benzene. In the frozen epoch-45 prior records, the 236 aromatic argmax molecules contained 252 perceived aromatic rings, of which 198 had exclusively raw AROMATIC edges, 51 mixed explicit aromatic and ordinary edges, and three had only single bonds. Across categorical draws the corresponding ring counts were 129, 57 and three. All six ordinary-only cases were three-membered rings. Inspecting the saved edge labels on each perceived ring found zero raw DOUBLE edges across all 441 prior ring occurrences. In the reconstruction panel, all 19,263 perceived aromatic rings consisted entirely of raw AROMATIC edges. These counts compare edges in their original decoder atom slots before and after sanitization. [Raw ring audit and examples](../../outputs/runs/aromaticity-20261118/vae/raw-ring-bond-encodings.json)

## How representative are the BRD4 results?

A target-specific reference supports the concern. ChEMBL returned 315 human BRD4 binding-assay measurements with reported Kd ≤100 nM, exact relation, and potential duplicates excluded. One record lacked a structure. Deduplicating the rest by stereo-free, connected structure gave **187 compounds, all 187 aromatic**. All 40 with 20–30 heavy atoms were aromatic. An assay-description subset explicitly mentioning BD1 and excluding BD2 or paired-domain labels contained **71 distinct molecules, all with at least two aromatic rings**. The bound 3MXF reference ligand JQ1 has four RDKit rings, three aromatic. The ChEMBL panel counts a class of experimentally characterized compounds and related chemical series. It measures neither universal ligand prevalence nor the necessity of aromaticity for binding. Across the seven reference ligands of the separate Boltz target panel, five had an aromatic ring and two had none. ## Where the problem enters

- **The scarcity starts before scoring.** Epoch 45 can reconstruct aromatic targets and generate benzene and heteroaromatic rings, yet only 3.13% of valid prior argmax decodes contain one. The graph schema round-trips benzene, pyridine and thiophene. Neutral pyrrole, imidazole and indole failed the same check. The graph omits explicit hydrogen counts, which matters for some `[nH]` chemotypes. This is a narrower coverage issue alongside the gap for supported rings. [Prior/real comparison][vae] · [Schema probe][schema]
- **Search and ranking both deserve attention.** Conventional aromatic candidates reach fresh scoring, but the best-ranked adaptive candidates are overwhelmingly nonaromatic. The geometry-off Protenix bond feature receives bond existence and discards the distinction among single, double and aromatic bonds at fixed connectivity. The VAE, chemistry guidance, atom features, and any rebuilt hard geometry can still affect the result. The observed ranks do not isolate a causal penalty on aromaticity. [Feature contract](../../../mosaic/src/mosaic/small_molecule/README.md) · [Fresh ranks][matched]
- **The next diagnostic should separate formation, retention and scoring.** Pair held-out aromatic and nonaromatic 25-heavy-atom founders by size, chemistry and starting score. Give the existing adaptive policy equal calls. Measure formation on nonaromatic starts, retention of the original mapped aromatic ring on aromatic starts, and fresh and held-out rank for both. In parallel, use the same prior vectors at training checkpoints to report valid five-/six-membered aromatics, heteroaromatics and two-ring yield. This would distinguish a generator fix from a scoring fix before adding any aromaticity reward.

## Direct hallucination without a VAE

Mosaic optimized the atom and bond probabilities themselves. Even with this change of representation, the 1,390 completed Stage 3 runs generated **0 conventional five- or six-membered aromatic rings in 13,010 distinct valid molecules**. A second pair of 20,000-update direct Protenix runs found zero aromatics in 128 distinct valid archived molecules with passive archiving and zero in 20 with endpoint feedback. The two studies used different starts and search rules. [Stage 3 audit][mosaic] · [Long-run archives](../../outputs/runs/aromaticity-20261118/direct-followup/other/summary.json)

The raw bond labels localize the failure in the selected fixed-weight 0.5 DeFoG arm. Six of its nine starts were single-bond trees or fragments. The other three were dense random graphs with **70, 68 and 59 explicit aromatic-labelled edges** at step zero. Those scattered labels did not form valid aromatic starting molecules. Across all nine runs and **all 4,500 subsequent updates**, no hard argmax graph contained even one bond labelled DOUBLE or AROMATIC, including graphs that failed sanitization. This arm included a chemistry teacher, and used no VAE. [Initial tensors and per-run counters](../../outputs/runs/aromaticity-20261118/direct-followup/initials/results.json) · [All nine raw counts][mosaic]

The 2D Protenix contact pathway receives atom types and bond existence, computed as `1 − P(NO_BOND)`. Exchanging probability between single and aromatic on an existing edge gives the contact scorer identical inputs when connectivity and other features are held fixed. A local 25-heavy-atom control replaced a phenyl ring with a cyclohexyl ring on the same scaffold. The two softened graphs gave identical element and bond-existence arrays. One application of the campaign's connected chemistry projection preserved each input's hard bond labels, including the supplied phenyl ring. This establishes that an aromatic state can be represented and can survive this isolated projection. Formation and retention over native optimization remain separate questions. [Feature contract](../../../mosaic/stage2/README.md) · [Model-free paired control](../../outputs/runs/aromaticity-20261118/direct-followup/ring-order.json)

Could independent sampling expose aromatic molecules hidden by argmax? On the eight completed matched starts, we sampled 256 hard graphs each from the saved final probabilities and from the best-valid source probabilities for both control and teacher. Atom and unordered bond rows were drawn independently, at the stored probabilities, using matched uniforms and seed 20261118. We verified the argmax identities against all 32 archived records. **Zero of 8,192 draws were both connected and sanitized**, including draws from 16 source states with valid argmax molecules. These particular stochastic readouts failed at feasibility, so they cannot estimate aromaticity conditional on validity. [Final-state draws](../../outputs/runs/aromaticity-20261118/direct-followup/endpoint-readouts.json) · [Best-valid source draws](../../outputs/runs/aromaticity-20261118/direct-followup/best-readouts.json) · [Replay script](../../outputs/runs/aromaticity-20261118/direct-followup/read_endpoints.py)

For the next direct-only comparison, use matched aromatic and saturated versions of a closed six-carbon ring on the same scaffold. Keep the model, pocket, projection and call budget fixed. Track whether the aromatic start retains the ring and whether the saturated start can change its bond orders. A comparator that explicitly proposes a consistent ring-wide bond-order edit supplies a positive control for generation and fresh rescoring. This isolates bond-order changes from the harder task of first closing a ring.

## Reproduction

This was an offline chemistry analysis of saved model outputs, plus the frozen public BRD4/CCD records. It used the 10,000-draw and 10,000-reconstruction epoch-45 panels, all 864 matched search windows across three seeds and three methods, and the complete raw valid readouts from the 1,390 completed original Mosaic runs. No new training, optimization or native scoring was needed. The analysis scripts accompany their exact input snapshots. [VAE script](../../outputs/runs/aromaticity-20261118/vae/audit_vae.py) · [Adaptive script](../../outputs/runs/aromaticity-20261118/matched/analyze.py) · [Mosaic script](../../outputs/runs/aromaticity-20261118/mosaic/audit.py)

To replay the reference and additional archive counts from the DBMol repository root, with RDKit installed and a new output directory:

```sh
python outputs/runs/aromaticity-20261118/reference-and-archives/reference_and_archives.py --restart-root PATH_TO_RESTART_WORKTREE --public-inputs outputs/runs/aromaticity-20261118/reference-and-archives/public-inputs.json --output NEW_OUTPUT
```
