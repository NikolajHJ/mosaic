## Offload conditions and accounting

The most plausible low-dependency categories are already redundant source upload archives beside independently authenticated source trees, and obsolete owned pytest caches/disposable fixture directories outside every current proof map. The parent must still verify exact current job/open-file dependencies, complete laptop backups and every file/member, then compare actual remote bytes again before removal. This review supplies no deletion allowlist.

Use remote `lstat` device/inode/link count/mode/size/allocated-block metadata. Two paths may share one hardlinked inode; removing one can free no physical data. Sparse files and quota refresh also prevent logical byte totals from establishing physical savings. The conservative gate subtracts stale scope in addition to raw quota accounting; changing that scope can affect the next admission calculation. Rerun the unchanged mandatory whole-campaign gate after a completed, audited removal rather than estimating a pass from aggregate `du`.

Do not remove old failures, input bindings, source trees, current CPU/native/startup/readiness proofs, unknown-outcome markers, environment/model assets or another project's files. Complete original scientific results must remain intact in verified backups even when an eligible remote duplicate is eventually removed by the parent operator.
