05 / METHOD
InitializationFixed occupancy and charge.
Update ruleSimplex projection; initial step size 0.01 with up to 10 halving trials.
AcceptanceBoth primal and AD-evaluated losses decrease by more than 10⁻⁷.

Numerical checks

Initial atom and bond-existence gradients are checked against finite differences. Null bond-order controls, scalar primal/AD parity and frozen model/context identities are checked. Highest FP32 matrix precision is used.

Scope of interpretation

Optimization acts on shared relaxed atom and edge probabilities. It does not optimize occupancy, formal charge or bond-order-specific native features. Sanitized readouts are not evidence of binding, synthesis feasibility or physical relaxation.
