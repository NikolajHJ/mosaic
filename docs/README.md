## Which statements apply to you

- Reusable guides use your identity and authorized storage. They do not grant access to another user's work, home, environment, model cache or private evidence.
- Experiment documents mix protocols, plans and dated observations. Read their dates and latest saved evidence; early preparation text is not a current scheduler result. Keep exact historical paths/hashes and failed outcomes.
- Many canonical launchers/controllers still contain account-specific constants. The guide documents that limitation; placeholder examples do not configure them.
- The [original project PDF](project_desc.pdf) is a design-stage brief. Its statements about missing functionality are not a description of today's implementation; use the [scope map](project_reference_status.md).
- The [OneNote export](onenote_export/README.md) preserves imported lab notes and attachments, including named-user examples. It is not a current Mosaic runbook or a certification of external lab/HR/grant/financial policy.

## Keep the distinction clear

Write new setup examples with an operator-selected SSH identity, remote `$HOME`, authorized scratch/project root and actual runtime/cache. A conventional `/work3/<username>` is a location to verify, not a guaranteed allocation. Never globally replace account names inside sealed source or evidence. A new-account port requires its own source/config/input/runtime identities and applicable allocated checks.
