## 1. Research question and limits of the claim

The immediate question is whether a learned graph decoder supplies a useful
continuous search space for molecular optimization while preserving valid,
discrete candidates. The AE/VAE comparison asks what latent regularization adds
to reconstruction, local search, and downstream optimization under comparable
conditions.

The intended molecular size is an output of the decoder. A fixed slot capacity
`N_max` bounds representable molecules; it is not an instruction to occupy exactly
that many atoms. Size control remains an explicit part of the design objective.

A decoder is a learned chemical prior, not a proof of chemical validity,
synthesizability, binding, or selectivity. These are separate evaluation questions.
Likewise, a gradient through a relaxed molecule does not establish that its
discretized counterpart retains the improvement.

## 3. Shared molecular graph contract

For a batch size `B`, use a dense graph with capacity `N_max = 50`. The confirmed
initial scope is a heavy-atom graph. Molecules that cannot survive the exact
graph round trip because hydrogen, isotope, radical or other necessary information
is not represented are rejected explicitly; retention must be audited before
training.

| Tensor | Shape | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `X` | `[B, N_max, K_atom]` | Atom-category probabilities, including `NO_ATOM` |
| `Q_prob` | `[B, N_max, 5]` | Formal-charge probabilities over `(-2, -1, 0, 1, 2)` |
| `E` | `[B, N_max, N_max, K_bond]` | Bond-category probabilities |
| `M_capacity` | `[B, N_max]`, if required | Slots available to the model; distinct from current occupancy |
| `p` | `[B, N_max]` | Derived occupancy, `1 - X[..., NO_ATOM]` |

Charge is categorical in this initial implementation. The Boltz2 adapter may form
the expected scalar charge `Q = sum_c Q_prob[..., c] * charge_value[c]` if needed
by its continuous input. Hard evaluation recovers a discrete charge category.
Document these distinct representations rather than silently changing the
meaning of `Q` between stages. The element vocabulary has ten categories,
including `NO_ATOM`.

The confirmed bond vocabulary is `NONE, SINGLE, DOUBLE, TRIPLE, AROMATIC`; `NONE`
is the no-bond category. Treatment of unusual bonds and handling of aromaticity
are part of the data manifest. Aromatic bonds cannot be assumed to form a chemically
consistent aromatic system merely because every individual category is allowed.

### Occupancy and size

The expected heavy-atom count is

```text
p_i = 1 - X[i, NO_ATOM]
N_eff = sum_i p_i
```

For future variable-size optimization, initially unoccupied slots must remain
usable for atom birth. This is a future design requirement; the implemented
Boltz engineering adapter deliberately fixes seed occupancy. A fixed mask derived
from the starting molecule must not permanently disable them. If a capacity mask
is used for batching, explain exactly which slots it excludes and why.

Charges, bonds, messages, pooling and any scoring contribution involving absent
atoms must use consistent occupancy semantics. For example, pair contributions
can use `p_i * p_j`. Merely multiplying a final score by occupancy does not remove
all routes by which a fractional or absent atom can influence intermediate states.
Document the actual gating once implemented, including where it is applied and
how fully occupied one-hot inputs agree with ordinary discrete inputs.

Size penalties may use `N_eff`, but evaluation must also report the realized
discrete atom count. A range alone can still encourage all candidates to reach its
upper bound. The size objective is therefore separate from the slot capacity.

### Bonds and other invariants

- Maintain `E[i, j] == E[j, i]` by construction, for example by predicting one
  triangle and mirroring it. The initial architecture uses a symmetric pair MLP;
  record and test the exact symmetry construction.
- Force diagonal edges to `NONE`.
- Preserve normalized categorical outputs at the relaxed decoder boundary.
- In the discrete graph, slots decoded as `NO_ATOM` have no incident bonds and
  contribute no formal charge.
- Explicitly check nonempty output, connectedness when required, and chemical
  sanitization. Do not silently discard fragments or repair graphs while reporting
  raw decoder validity.
- Stereochemistry is explicitly removed for this initial topology-only prototype.
  Deduplicate after its removal and do not report reconstruction or novelty as
  stereochemically exact.

Chemical parsing, sanitization, canonicalization and route planning belong to
data preparation or discrete evaluation unless a documented gradient estimator
is introduced. Their presence in the overall workflow does not make them
differentiable.

## 4. Graph ordering and the reconstruction target

An unordered molecule admits many atom indexings. A node-wise cross-entropy loss
compares index `i` with index `i`; it does not automatically compare molecules
up to graph isomorphism. This is a central architecture decision, not a logging
detail.

Two possible strategies are:

1. **Deterministic ordered targets.** Use a pinned, deterministic atom-ordering
   procedure and have the decoder reconstruct that convention. Store the mapping
   from source atoms to training slots. A permutation-invariant encoder and an
   ordered decoder can then reconstruct the canonical target, but should not be
   described as reconstructing arbitrary supplied index order.
2. **Alignment during reconstruction.** Match predicted nodes to target nodes
   before scoring the loss, with an explicit treatment of symmetry, empty slots,
   bonds and charge. Record the matching algorithm and its approximation. A
   matching operation can be used to choose a loss target without implying that
   the discrete assignment itself has ordinary derivatives.

The selected initial strategy is **deterministic ordered targets via canonical
SMILES round-trip ordering**, with position embeddings in the graph encoder and
an ordered slot/pair decoder. This makes the intended slot correspondence
explicit; the position-aware encoder should not be claimed to be permutation
invariant. Record the exact RDKit procedure/version and its treatment of symmetry.
Alignment-based reconstruction is an alternative, not part of the confirmed
initial implementation. Do not combine random atom-order augmentation with an
unaligned index-wise target unless the architecture or target transformation
actually supports it.

Evaluate exact molecule reconstruction using a declared canonical identity
policy in addition to slot-level accuracy. Symmetric atoms may change index
without changing molecular identity. Conversely, high per-slot accuracy can
coexist with a chemically important bond error.

size distribution, repair frequency, uniqueness, score retained after decoding,
and computational cost. Use matched starts and budgets when comparing AE/VAE or
objective variants. A conditional metric over valid candidates must show its
valid count alongside the original number of attempts.

Selection policies must be stated: final step, best valid checkpoint according
to an optimization-time criterion, or another prespecified rule. Held-out
evaluation should not quietly become the checkpoint-selection objective.

## 10. Reproducibility and handoff

Every execution, including tests and failed jobs, receives a ledger entry with
a unique run/attempt identity. Store resolved configs, seeds, data/code hashes,
environment versions, scheduler job IDs, stdout/stderr, checkpoints, metrics and
error records as described in the ledger. Record the actual resources and elapsed
time, not only the resource request.

No test, training or optimization result has been verified by the documentation
owner at creation of this document. The ledger must be updated from execution
evidence supplied by the coordinator; future intended behavior must not be
rewritten in the past tense before that evidence exists.
