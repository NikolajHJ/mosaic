September 26 – November 25, 2026

The molecular VAE training timeline

The first model learned from 4,000 ZINC molecules. The current lineage began with fresh weights on MOSES on October 11, continued on MOSES–ChEMBL, then moved to a 60-million-molecule training view. The durable idea was the interface. Each version decodes one molecular latent into probabilities for atoms, charges and bonds.

The most revealing divergence came in epoch 47. Exact reconstruction improved while 249 of 256 fixed random draws filled every atom slot. Since then we have tracked what the model generates and whether it can recognize its own generated molecules alongside reconstruction.

Reading the numbers. Exact reconstruction asks whether an encoded input comes back as the same molecule. Prior validity starts from random Gaussian vectors. Argmax takes the largest probability for every decision. Categorical sampling draws the decisions. Compare percentages only when the panel, decoding rule and denominator match. Boltz, DeFoG and Protenix latent-design experiments use a frozen VAE and have their own records.

ZINC prototypes

Sep 26–27Precursor
Data · Model · Loss

First AE and VAE on 5,000 ZINC molecules

A seeded subset of ZINC250K gave 4,000 training, 500 validation and 500 test structures. Both models encoded a graph into 128 numbers and decoded it in one pass with 128 features per atom. Atom type, formal charge and symmetric bond type were supervised separately. Empty atom slots let the decoded size vary. The VAE added Gaussian posterior sampling and KL to graph reconstruction and the size penalty.

At the end of the small 60-epoch runs, exact validation reconstruction on the 256-molecule panel was 1 for the AE and 0 for the VAE.

Data · Evaluation

Scale the same comparison to 215,000 training molecules

Filtering and deduplication left 229,694 usable ZINC structures. The split was 215,000 training and 7,347 each for validation and test. The AE initially had the advantage on exact reconstruction. A separate paired path study gave the VAE a reason to stay in contention.

The selected AE reconstructed 7,032/7,347 test molecules exactly and the VAE 6,511/7,347. Only 13/1,024 Gaussian draws from the VAE were valid. On 223 paths with valid endpoints for both models, the VAE had an 11.51 percentage-point advantage in intermediate validity.

Sep 28–Oct 1Precursor
Model

From one-shot predictions to persistent bond states

The architecture sweep increased the molecular latent to 256, narrowed atom states to 96 and gave each possible bond a 48-feature hidden state. Two rounds let atoms and pairs exchange information. Other arms tried a deeper trunk and a molecule-wide bond context with a direct path from the latent. An unstable deep VAE failed at epoch 7. A stabilized version used normalization in the residual path.

The pair-refinement VAE reached 7,103/7,347 exact but only 18/1,024 valid prior draws. The stabilized VAE reached 7,159 exact. Continuing the wide VAE from 120 to 180 epochs still gave just 8/1,024 valid prior draws at its selected checkpoint.

Oct 7–10Precursor
Loss · Sampling

The chemistry loss discovers a sulfur shortcut

A separate experiment froze the encoder of the stabilized ZINC VAE at its epoch-89 checkpoint. After testing probability-aware repair targets, a dense chemistry experiment trained its decoder on batches of 64 encoded molecules and 64 fresh Gaussian points. Real examples also received reconstruction supervision.

After 100 decoder-only epochs, valid prior argmax graphs rose from 64 to 840 of 4,096. But expected sulfur atoms per prior graph rose from 0.39 to 17.58. This informed later atom-preserving repair and composition checks. The MOSES model inherited no weights from this run.

Rebuild on MOSES

Oct 11–13Main lineage
Data · Model · Loss

Fresh initialization starts the current weight lineage

The supported MOSES split contained 1,688,539 training molecules and 20,000 each for validation and test. A new 3,941,860-parameter VAE trained encoder and decoder jointly from scratch. Four bond-aware encoder layers fed a 320-wide residual trunk and a 256D latent. The decoder projected directly into 50 × 96 atom states, then used four residual atom–pair rounds with triangle updates and four-head atom attention. Real graphs received reconstruction and KL. The old explicit size penalty was set to zero.

At epoch 25, 4,088/4,096 reconstructions were valid, while only 47/4,096 Gaussian argmax decodes were valid. Real-only training stopped at epoch 35, update 230,860.

Oct 18–19 · E36Main lineage
Loss · Sampling

Train the decoder explicitly on random latent points

From epoch 35, train on 256 real molecules and 512 Gaussian points per update. A detached, probability-aware search repaired bonds while holding atom identity, occupancy and charge fixed. The live decoder learned the returned targets. Batch composition regularization discouraged another sulfur shortcut, and the frozen epoch-35 decoder anchored slot occupancy. Search workers ran alongside GPU training. The planned 1:3 ratio missed the runtime target, so production used 1:2.

Epoch 36 took 9.68 hours. On the fixed monitors, 641/1,024 prior argmax decodes were valid and 256/256 real molecules reconstructed exactly.

A decreasing loss can hide a collapsing prior

An attempted epoch 37 was stopped before promotion. Experiments tested deeper repair, a fallback for failed searches, weaker self-supervision on already-valid graphs, smaller updates and changes to the no-bond weight. Fixed latent panels exposed a failure that individual gradient checks had missed.

One 100-update replay went from 641 to 674 to 357 valid draws out of 1,024. At one damaging update, the real reconstruction gradient on the decoder was about 28 times the prior-repair gradient. A live self-target also disappeared as soon as its own graph became invalid.

Loss · Optimizer

Freeze a good target at the same latent

The accepted continuation kept the epoch-35 occupancy anchor and added a frozen epoch-36 teacher. When the teacher produced a valid, representable graph at a random point, that graph remained a training target even if the student had drifted. The frozen-target NLL coefficient was 0.1. AdamW continued updating its moments, but only a quarter of each proposed parameter displacement was applied.

A 2,500-update replay reached 665/1,024 valid prior graphs with 256/256 exact reconstructions. Production reached 753/1,024 prior-valid at epoch 40 and continued through epoch 43. Different rows in this report use different panels.

Train around known codes as well as in the prior

The logical batch kept 256 real molecules. After a ramp, its chemistry examples comprised 384 independent Gaussian draws and 128 perturbations around encoded real molecules. Structural proposals speed up the search for a repair

The search tried edits suggested by disconnected components and valence violations before falling back to beam search. On the recorded diagnostic it repaired 123/123 cases in 2.82 s, versus 122/123 in 10.29 s for beam search. The full epoch still took about ten hours.

The 60M pass and the 50-atom failure

Nov 16–19 · E47Main lineage
Data · Sampling · Loss

Real reconstruction improved as random generation collapsed

To make a 60M pass affordable, each update used 240 real molecules for reconstruction and KL, and 16 fresh priors for chemistry. By update 524177, the model had seen 47,682,960 rows. On a fixed PubChem panel, exact reconstruction rose from 395 to 480 out of 512.

On the same 256 prior vectors, raw validity fell from 190 to 6 and full 50-slot graphs rose from 0 to 249. The repair objective could reinforce the model’s own occupied-slot decisions. The exact update that initiated the collapse is unknown.

Nov 21–22 · E48Main lineage
Model · Loss · Sampling

Seven refinement rounds, output graph matching and cheap chemistry on every prior

The seven-round arm added three independently weighted triangle and typed-bond-message rounds to the original four. The model grew from 3,941,860 to 4,301,290 parameters. Reconstruction aligned predicted and target graphs, allowing correct renumberings. On 240 priors per 240 real examples, a detached valence projection trained changed bonds; 16 priors also received frozen-teacher supervision.

The seven-round arm also introduced a weak batch loss for valid aromatic rings (8%) and amides (12%). Its first leg went from 332 to 475 valid argmax priors out of 512 and from 244 to 247 exact held-out reconstructions out of 256. Depth and motif supervision changed together.

Teach the encoder to recognize its own generated molecules

Every fourth update the trainer selected up to eight valid, canonically representable generated parents from 16 priors. It encoded each graph, decoded its posterior mean and supervised recovery of the original graph with weight 0.10. This branch froze the decoder so its gradients trained the encoder. The other branches continued joint training.

Fixed-parent and fresh-parent panels began measuring exact identity as well as invented and missing bonds. A valid random molecule and the same molecule after re-encoding had proved to be quite different tests.
