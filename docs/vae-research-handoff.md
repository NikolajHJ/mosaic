**Evidence through 19 November 2026.** This is a working reference for an agent picking up the molecular VAE in DBMol_exploratory or integrating its decoder into Mosaic. It follows the project from its first autoencoders to the 60-million-molecule run, including experiments that failed or stayed exploratory. For a group meeting, use the [two-page illustrated briefing](../outputs/reports/molecular-vae-research-journey.pdf). For decisions about the code, follow the linked configs, experiment records and saved outputs. Some raw artifacts live in the workspace's ignored `outputs/` directory or on DTU and may be absent from a fresh clone.

## Where the project stands

We set out to optimize a small molecule by moving one continuous vector. The decoder turns that vector into probabilities over a variable-size molecular graph. A frozen protein scorer can then differentiate a binding or contact objective through the decoder to the vector. An encoded starting ligand and a fresh Gaussian draw give two very different starting conditions. We ultimately want both to yield chemically meaningful edits, including changes in size, with improvements that survive fresh scoring of the resulting discrete molecule.

## Representation and model

The schema is a heavy-atom 2D graph with a maximum of 50 slots. Its element categories in saved order are empty, C, N, O, F, P, S, Cl, Br and I. Formal charges are −2, −1, 0, +1 and +2. Bond categories are none, single, double, triple and aromatic. The schema uses implicit hydrogens and drops stereochemistry in the canonical molecular identity. The latent therefore cannot specify an enantiomer or a 3D pose. A scorer that needs coordinates must obtain them from a separate declared source. Read category order from the checkpoint spec when mapping into another model. [GraphSpec and graph tensors](../src/latentmol/representation.py) · [Encoding, hard validity and identity](../src/latentmol/chemistry.py)

**There are three useful views of the decoder output.** Confusing them has direct consequences for atom count, bond probability and gradients.

| View | Shapes for one molecule | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raw logits (`DecoderOutput`) | Atom `[50,10]`, charge `[50,5]`, bond `[50,50,5]` | Cross-entropy, stable occupancy logits, conditional bond probabilities. The shared bond view averages both orientations before softmax. |
| Differentiable marginals (`RelaxedGraph`) | Same category axes, with normalized probabilities | Passing continuous features to a scorer. Slot occupancy is one minus its empty probability. Off-diagonal conditional bond probabilities are gated by the product of both endpoint occupancies. Missing mass goes to no bond. |
| Discrete graph (`HardGraphBatch`) | One atom and charge label per slot and one bond label per pair | Exact reconstruction, chemical validation, detached repair targets and fresh scoring. The standard argmax path takes conditional bond argmax and applies the hard occupied-pair mask. |

Thus a mostly empty slot can have a plausible **conditional** bond type while its **marginal** chance of a bond remains low. Passing the marginal into an adapter and multiplying by both endpoint occupancies again changes the objective. Conversely, using the conditional prediction as a real edge to an absent atom changes the represented graph. The hardening rule also matters for ambiguous slots. The most likely individual category can be empty even when the *sum* of probabilities of all real elements exceeds 0.5. Report any alternative occupancy threshold explicitly. [Conversion and hardening](../src/latentmol/representation.py) · [Categorical sampling](../src/latentmol/sampling.py)

### The large triangle VAE

```text
hard molecular graph, in the encoder's expected slot order
    -> 4 bond-aware encoder message blocks, atom width 96
    -> flatten 50 x 96 -> 320 -> 2 residual 320-wide updates
    -> 256 means and 256 log variances
    -> sample z for training, or use the posterior mean to reconstruct

z, 256 coordinates
    -> direct projection to 50 x 96 atom states
    -> initialize symmetric 50 x 50 x 48 pair states
    -> 4 separately parameterized residual refinement rounds
       pair update + gated triangle paths + pair-aware atom attention
       soft atom and charge feedback into subsequent computations
    -> atom / charge / symmetric bond logits
    -> relaxed graph for gradients, discrete graph for chemical evaluation
```

The encoder accounts for **2,173,216** parameters and the decoder **1,768,644**, for **3,941,860** total. The production architecture uses a direct latent-to-slot projection. Earlier global trunks, global bond-context vectors and wider hidden layers describe earlier comparisons. Each large-model round has four attention heads, a 96-wide atom update, a pair MLP with 96-wide hidden layers and a 24-wide triangle bottleneck. A pair state is a feature vector for a possible relationship. Its width 48 is independent of the number of atom slots and the five eventual bond labels. [Architecture and confirmed counts](52_large_corpus_triangle_vae.md) · [Encoder](../src/latentmol/models/encoder.py) · [Refinement and heads](../src/latentmol/models/refined_decoder.py)

For pair `(i,j)`, the triangle update uses learned projections of `(i,k)` and `(k,j)` through intermediate slots `k`. It gates by occupancy, excludes self-paths, normalizes by eligible intermediate occupancy and symmetrizes the result. The implementation contracts pair tensors directly. It can express that two candidate bonds meeting at a third atom inform another pair. This supplies context before the final categorical choices. Separate draws from those heads can still combine into an invalid graph. [TrianglePairUpdate](../src/latentmol/models/triangle.py)

During VAE training a sampled encoding is `z = mu + exp(logvar / 2) * epsilon`. Reconstruction gradients can reach both encoder and decoder. The standard KL is summed over latent dimensions and averaged across molecules. In the large real-molecule branch, cross-entropy supervises every atom slot with reduced padding weight, charge over occupied atoms, and bonds over occupied upper-triangular pairs. The effective size penalty comes from the checkpoint/config for the experiment. The partial-47 run used zero explicit real size-loss weight even though the shared loss class has a nonzero default. During a design run that optimizes one latent, the decoder and scorer remain frozen and only that vector is stepped. [Shared model](../src/latentmol/models/autoencoder.py) · [Reconstruction reductions and KL](../src/latentmol/training/losses.py) · [Epoch-47 objective audit](../experiments/vae/occupancy-collapse.md)

## Reading the metrics

| Metric or event | How to read it |
| --- | --- |
| Exact reconstruction | Compare the graph decoded from a known molecule with the original under the declared graph identity. The main held-out tables typically compare canonical, stereo-stripped identities after posterior-mean argmax. Compact graph-mode evaluation additionally recognizes verified label-preserving slot permutations. |
| Valid reconstruction | A known molecule was encoded and its output satisfies the chemistry checks. A different valid molecule can pass. Always report exact reconstruction separately. |
| Unconditional raw validity | Draw a fresh latent from the declared prior and decode. Count every attempted latent, including empty, disconnected and unsanitizable outputs. State the decoding law and whether there are multiple categorical draws per latent. |
| Argmax versus categorical generation | Argmax picks the most likely label at each decision and handles padding. Categorical decoding draws from the distributions and samples each undirected bond once. Different validity on the same latents is expected. Phase-specific temperatures, rejection sampling or repairing afterward change the question. |
| Raw versus strict teacher validity | Native inspection checks graph labels, RDKit sanitization, a single component and radical status. Strict teacher eligibility additionally checks serialized SMILES and representability through the project's round trip. The first failing stage provides a diagnostic category. |
| Repair coverage or cost | Coverage is the fraction of attempted candidates with the declared repair outcome. Keep already-valid, repaired, censored and errored counts separate. A finite search cost is a cost to a witnessed repair in that budget. A missing witness leaves the true nearest valid graph unknown. |
| Diversity | Record distinct valid canonical identities relative to all attempts, and scaffold diversity when available. Hundreds of sanitized copies of a small family can look strong in a validity-only summary. |
| Size and occupancy | For a raw hard graph count occupied slots. For the relaxed graph sum the slot occupancy probabilities. Report the distribution, median and fraction hitting 50. A mean can hide a minority stuck at the ceiling. Counts after a fixed-size selection or repair describe that procedure. |
| Scorer improvement | A relaxed score tests the differentiable surrogate. A fresh score on the discrete candidate tests transfer through hardening and any geometry rebuild. Predicted contact, affinity and confidence have different units and directions. None measures experimental activity. |

These quantities also have different denominators. A training window of 1,000 updates at 16 fresh priors per update contains 16,000 varying draws. A fixed independent panel might have only 128 shared latents. The first can flag trouble quickly, while the second supports paired comparisons. Similarly, a study sampling four hard graphs from each latent has more graphs than independent latent draws. Save denominators, seeds, membership, failure categories, temperature and use of repair next to each claimed result. ## Early experiments

### What the first experiments selected for

The early ZINC experiments compared a deterministic graph autoencoder (AE) with a variational autoencoder (VAE). An AE assigns a code to each input graph. A VAE predicts a Gaussian mean and log variance for each input. During training the sampled code is the mean plus Gaussian noise scaled by the predicted standard deviation. Both learn categorical graph reconstruction. The VAE adds a KL penalty against a standard Gaussian prior. This supplies a simple distribution to sample during generation. Whether that prior reaches parts of the decoder that produce valid, diverse, editable molecules is an empirical question. [Training losses](../src/latentmol/training/losses.py) · [Historical architecture comparison](29_architecture_comparison_20261001.md)

The ZINC split contained 215,000 training, 7,347 validation and 7,347 test molecules after filtering unsupported representations and duplicates. Selection took place on validation before evaluation on the random held-out split. One training seed and different stopping epochs limit comparisons. The pair-refinement VAE reconstructed **7,103/7,347** held-out molecules exactly, compared with **7,064/7,347** for its AE counterpart. Yet only **18/1,024** standard-normal draws from that VAE decoded to valid molecules. Across VAE variants with results the corresponding range was **8–18/1,024**. Drawing several categorical outputs at a code obtained by encoding a real molecule often reproduced that molecule. Conditional decoding around known inputs and generating from a new random vector should therefore have separate columns in any results table. [ZINC split, checkpoint selection and comparisons](29_architecture_comparison_20261001.md) · [Fixed-code sampling protocol](25_architecture_results_20260929.md)

| Early decoder experiment | What the evidence supports |
| --- | --- |
| Residual and pair refinements | The residual VAE reconstructed 6,587/7,347 test molecules exactly. Pair refinement increased that count to 7,103 on its own validation-selected checkpoint. Each saw the same split. |
| Separate global bond context | A path from the molecular latent to the bond calculations bypassed the atom states. The wide AE reached 7,169/7,347 exact. Its place in the study was as a strong reconstruction comparator. |
| Additional depth and stability | The original deep-pair VAE failed in epoch 7. A separately trained stabilized deep-pair variant reached 7,159/7,347 exact. At equal epoch 60, the stabilized VAE reconstructed 6,920 exactly versus 5,313 for the wide VAE. These families have different parameter counts. |
| Continue the wide VAE | Restoring the model, optimizer and random streams allowed the 120-epoch campaign to reach 180 epochs. Across the two validation-selected checkpoints, exact test reconstruction increased from 6,890 to 6,948. The latter produced 8/1,024 valid prior draws. More steps helped reconstruction while generation remained poor. |

The full table names checkpoint roles separately. For example, the epoch-86 stabilized VAE won its validation-reconstruction comparison. An epoch-89 stabilized checkpoint selected for a different role became the parent of later decoder-only work. Substituting one changes the experiment even when architecture names match. [Six-family results](29_architecture_comparison_20261001.md) · [Wide continuation config](../configs/arch_wide_global_bond_vae_continue180.yaml) · [Stabilized family config](../configs/arch_stable_deep_pair_refine_vae_full.yaml)

There was a reason to keep a VAE alongside better deterministic reconstruction baselines. An early 128-dimensional AE/VAE study found an **11.51 percentage-point** advantage for VAE intermediate validity on 223 paths where both models produced valid endpoints, with three intermediate attempts per path. Its anchor-clustered interval was 7.69–15.41 points. The same study tested a density estimate in latent space. On the VAE, optimizing with that estimate gave **49.65%** valid decodes versus **70.70%** for the local trust-region control. Being in a region of high empirical embedding density was an unreliable substitute for measuring graph quality in this setup. Neither result establishes semantic interpolation, stronger binding, or transfer to the later 256-dimensional representation. [Paired latent study](11_stage1_latent_study.md) · [Cross-study interpretation](29_architecture_comparison_20261001.md)

### Why we introduced a random training branch

The MOSES triangle VAE started with joint encoder/decoder training on **1,688,539** supported training molecules, plus 20,000 each for validation and test. Its initial objective was reconstruction plus KL with zero explicit size-loss weight. Gaussian posterior noise during reconstruction is distinct from drawing new vectors independently from the standard Gaussian. The latter appeared in evaluation during this phase. At epoch 25, **4,088/4,096** posterior-mean argmax reconstructions were chemically valid. Only **47/4,096** raw prior argmax graphs and **73/16,384** raw prior categorical draws were valid. The categorical observations came from four draws at each prior point. These results motivated explicit training around the prior. Simultaneous differences in data, architecture and training prevent attributing this trajectory to triangle updates alone. [MOSES corpus, original objective and epoch-25 measurements](52_large_corpus_triangle_vae.md)

## How the chemistry training objective evolved

### A successful proxy exposed a sulfur shortcut

In a separate decoder-only ZINC campaign, the stabilized epoch-89 encoder remained frozen. Dense chemistry supervision used posterior codes from real molecules and fresh Gaussian vectors. On 4,096 shared prior points the original decoder produced 64 valid argmax graphs. After 100 decoder-training epochs it produced **840/4,096**. Categorical validity rose from 124 to 2,743 of 16,384 draws and exact held-out reconstruction rose from 7,160 to 7,182 of 7,347. Meanwhile expected sulfur count per random graph rose from **0.391 to 17.58**, expected carbon fell from **16.48 to 3.68**, and expected heavy-atom count stayed near 22. These are soft population expectations over all draws. The model found a chemically unrepresentative way to improve validity. [Matched decoder evaluation](45_dense_chemistry_100_epoch_continuation.md)

The maximum-valence penalty explains an incentive in the objective. For a neutral atom with incident bond order five, its excess above a carbon cap of four is one, while its excess above a sulfur cap of six is zero. The optimizer can reduce this term merely by shifting element probability toward sulfur. The calculation establishes a direction favored by that component. Multiple terms share a decoder, so it does not establish what fraction of the measured drift came from that direction. Later neighbor-aware rules gave a second caution. In a matched local pilot from a common 823/1,024 valid start, a control retained 734 valid after ten updates while the added neighbor term retained only 611. Fewer local valence errors coincided with more disconnected graphs. The group kept that pilot out of the production recipe. [Sulfur mechanism audit](84_sulfur_bias_in_validity_guidance.md) · [Neighbor-loss pilot](88_local_neighbor_loss_pilot.md)

### A repair witness gives a gradient after the search

The triangle chemistry continuation started from epoch 35. For a student decode, the search took its argmax graph and held occupied slots, elements and formal charges fixed. It explored bond edits using detached decoder probabilities to prioritize candidates. A successful witness had to pass the configured graph, connectedness, chemistry and representation checks. Differentiating a categorical loss that increases the student’s probability of that witness requires no derivative through the search or chemistry toolkit. The initial target loss included **all** atom slots plus occupied charges and unordered occupied bond pairs. Thus a search that only *edits* bonds can still send a direct supervised gradient to atom logits. That distinction becomes important again at epoch 47. [Search and objective specification](53_atom_preserving_triangle_continuation.md) · [Repair target and loss implementation](../src/latentmol/atom_preserving_chemistry.py)

The objective combined real reconstruction and KL, real and synthetic repair terms, distributional element and charge penalties, and occupancy guidance on fresh random vectors from the frozen epoch-35 decoder at the same coordinates. A population composition penalty can limit widespread drift while leaving individual molecules compositionally distinct. Treating the dataset average as a mandatory formula for each small molecule would answer a different question. The campaign benchmarked 1 real to 3 random examples and alternative execution settings. The eventual frozen-reference validation from epoch 36 used **256 real and 512 prior examples**. Preserve coefficients and reduction rules separately from these counts. In particular, averaging a per-branch loss differs from pooling all graph decisions across both branches. [Weights and denominators](53_atom_preserving_triangle_continuation.md#loss-weighting-and-the-composition-prior) · [Actual reference-validation settings](74_frozen_valid_reference_guidance.md#exact-candidate-objective-and-identities)

The original teacher returned no repair target for already-valid graphs or for graphs without a witness inside the budget. It recorded those outcomes separately. The former is a valid raw output. The latter is censored search with unknown distance to the nearest valid graph. A subsequent experimental branch escalated bounded search and considered partial-progress signals for censored graphs. Keep raw validity, witness coverage among eligible graphs and the loss denominator separate when reading a study. In one saved epoch-36-branch training row, 15 of 330 eligible priors were censored, or 15 of 512 scheduled priors. Neither percentage is the chemical failure rate of the decoder on independent random draws. [Failure categories and measured denominators](63_failed_repair_guidance.md) · [Execution and runtime comparisons](66_failure_guidance_execution_speed.md)

### What “distance to validity” means in this implementation

For a search that edits a fixed set of graph labels, one supported witness cost is the number of changed labels plus a nonnegative probability-regret term. Changing a label from the raw choice with probability `p_old` to a label with probability `p_new` incurs `1 + w * max(0, log(p_old) - log(p_new))`, with a numerical probability floor. Changing one ambiguous bond from a 0.51 choice to its 0.49 alternative costs less than replacing a 0.999 choice with a 0.001 alternative. The implementation records edit count and regret separately. The search takes categorical decisions on a detached graph. The differentiable training loss scores returned labels under the live decoder. Minimizing a witnessed target's CE and minimizing the mathematical distance to the set of all valid graphs are different operations. [Witness cost and search](../src/latentmol/probability_repair.py) · [Search-gradient study](40_search_repair_gradient_study.md)

The search comparison on two frozen epoch-43 panels contained 512 prior graphs, including 123 invalid ones. Beam at radius three repaired 122/123 in 10.293 seconds of discovery work. Structural proposals with a beam fallback repaired 123/123 in 2.822 seconds. A* at radius three repaired 119/123 in 4.056 seconds, and on its 119 shared successes lowered mean witnessed cost from 2.7013 to 2.5457. The A* heuristic counts a lower bound on necessary edits from disconnected components and excess valence, then sums the cheapest available distinct bond-edit costs. That argument assumes fixed atoms and charges and one change per unordered bond. The frontier and edit-radius budgets can still cut off the search. A* improved some costs, while structural proposals provided the best coverage and time here. This is the basis for the epoch-45 choice. [Methods, costs and interruptions](../experiments/chemistry-guidance/README.md#probability-aware-a) · [A* implementation](../src/latentmol/guidance/repair_astar.py)

For comparing near-valid outputs offline, the default graded quality profile gives equal weight to local consistency, connectedness and repair proximity. It reports a 0–100 summary from the component scores. Other profiles exist and a missing positive-weight component can block a summary. An agent tuning the training loss should inspect the three underlying values and separately report the raw valid fraction. Report strict validity or valid chemical identity directly whenever an actual discrete molecule is required downstream. [Graded quality profiles](../src/latentmol/evaluation/quality.py)

### What the epoch-36 replays actually diagnosed

A replay reproduced historical fixed-panel raw validity of **641 → 674 → 357** out of 1,024 along the same 100 updates. At update 96 the full step lowered total loss yet moved valid count from 590 to 531. The reconstruction gradient norm on decoder parameters was roughly 28 times the prior-repair norm. A quarter-sized trial displacement from the same pre-step weights kept 580 valid, with fewer atom- and bond-label flips. This identifies a local objective tradeoff in shared parameters. It also explains why checking gradients for finite values, or observing decreasing total loss, could coexist with rapid loss of prior validity. Across 200 updates the quarter-step alone and a weak current-valid self-target both lost validity while each still reconstructed 256/256 held-out molecules exactly. [Replayed steps and independent self-target control](70_epoch36_collapse_mechanism.md)

Self-targeting had another weakness. Once a live graph became invalid, it ceased supplying the positive target that could preserve its earlier identity. Stronger self-targeting passed a short probe but failed longer monitoring. Changing the no-bond weighting and normalization produced a severe regression from 149 to 12 valid outputs on 256 fixed prior points in eight updates, despite passing derivative tests. A better source of positive labels was the frozen original epoch-36 model, evaluated at the same latent independently of the student’s current validity. The accepted new term used full categorical NLL for strictly valid frozen targets, weighted by 0.1 and averaged across all scheduled prior examples. The original frozen epoch-35 occupancy reference remained separate. [Self-guidance strength](71_valid_output_guidance_iteration.md) · [Bond-weighting regression](72_bond_weighted_target_guidance.md) · [Frozen reference and its objective](74_frozen_valid_reference_guidance.md)

After 2,500 disposable updates the frozen-reference version moved fixed-panel prior validity from **641 to 665 of 1,024**, categorical validity from **2,091 to 2,323 of 4,096**, and held exact reconstruction at **256/256**. Sulfur frequency went from 1.5070% to 1.5089%. The production continuation restarted from the saved epoch 36, preserving its optimizer history. It reached **753/1,024** valid at epoch 40 with 256/256 exact reconstruction. These longitudinal results give the reason to preserve the epoch-35 occupancy and epoch-36 target references when reproducing this particular campaign. [2,500-update proof](74_frozen_valid_reference_guidance.md) · [Production epoch-40 checkpoint](75_frozen_reference_epoch40_continuation.md)

### What carried through epoch 45

Epoch 44 changed both the fraction of the AdamW parameter displacement applied after updating the moments, from 0.25 to 0.125, and the synthetic batch composition. At full ramp a logical batch contained 256 real molecules, 384 independent prior draws and 128 local perturbations around real codes. The local branch used chemistry supervision. A local perturbation did not acquire the original molecule as a compulsory reconstruction target. Epoch 45 retained that mixture and added structural repair proposals. The proposals were 3.69 times faster in the serial CPU search benchmark, while complete epochs 44 and 45 took 9.94 and 9.97 hours. A faster search kernel can have little effect on total time once training, parallel search and evaluation share the allocation. [Local training protocol](89_latent_neighborhood_continuation.md) · [Structural search comparison](../experiments/chemistry-guidance/README.md) · [Completed training records](../experiments/vae/README.md)

On the fixed 1,024-point training monitor, epoch 45 rose to **769** raw valid graphs from epoch 44’s **759**. The larger, fresh comparison gives a better picture of the plateau. Across 10,000 prior draws the argmax valid rates at epochs 43, 44 and 45 were **75.49%, 74.88% and 75.41%**. Categorical valid rates were **64.35%, 64.26% and 63.30%**. On 10,000 real validation molecules exact reconstructions were **99.84%, 99.88% and 99.87%**. Use epoch 45 as a frozen reproducible starting point. Those measurements give little evidence that extending the same recipe for another few epochs would by itself solve generation or latent editability. ## Ordering, interpolation and data

### Atom ordering and reconstruction identity

A chemical graph survives a consistent renumbering of its atoms. Our original encoder flattened 50 ordered atom states before predicting the latent distribution. Changing input order could therefore change the vector and decoded molecule. Three questions need separate tests. Does a permutation of one input reproduce the same posterior? Does the resulting discrete graph equal the original molecule? Do corresponding atoms in **different** analogues occupy matching slots? Canonical ordering solved the first problem for supported inputs. The third still matters for interpolation. [Canonical pilot and raw comparisons](../experiments/vae/invariant-pilot.md)

We initially tried learned invariant pooling and a reconstruction objective that permitted atom matching. The first short pilot produced zero exact reconstructions on 2,048 held-out molecules. A follow-up added topology-sensitive messages and a longer encoder warm-up against the frozen ordered teacher. It still produced zero exact reconstructions. Finite, unclipped gradients and similar training and validation code errors gave little support for a simple clipping or held-out-overfitting explanation. A useful counterexample came from two chemically valid six-carbon graphs with different connectivity. Every node had degree three and identical initial features. Local messages and pooled moments gave the same vector for both, despite different triangle structure. This demonstrates a concrete information loss in that encoder family. Its frequency in the training set remains unknown. [Counterexample](../outputs/hpc-results/vae-canonical-invariant-pilot-20261103-a01/regular-cage-counterexample.json)

The successful correction maps each supported discrete graph into the dataset's canonical atom order **before** applying the trained ordered encoder. It copies the full encoder and decoder weights. The mapper establishes a bijection, appends empty slots, and verifies every element, charge and both axes of the bond matrix. Cached graphs may bypass repeated mapping after the cache convention, RDKit version and sampled canonical labels have been checked. Keep this discrete RDKit step outside the differentiable route from a live latent to a soft graph to a protein scorer. The encoder adapter explicitly rejects soft inputs or calls requiring gradients with respect to the input graph. [Mapping](../src/latentmol/chemistry.py) · [Encoder adapter and cache checks](../src/latentmol/datasets/ordering.py)

Job **29883264** ran 250 encoder-only and 750 joint updates. The parent and candidate reconstructed the same 2,044 of 2,048 held-out molecules exactly. All 1,024 shuffled trials reproduced their unshuffled candidate posteriors and outputs. Of those, 1,020 matched the original molecule, versus zero with the uncorrected parent on shuffled input. On this particular 10,000-draw panel, argmax validity was 75.55% for the parent and 75.48% for the candidate. Categorical validity was 63.05% and 63.04%. This justified integrating the canonical adapter and retaining the shuffled-input regression. It established neither global latent smoothness nor atom correspondence across analogues. [Matched outcome](../outputs/hpc-results/vae-canonical-invariant-pilot-20261103-a01/completed-comparison.json)

### What the interpolation experiments actually tested

Start with two real molecules A and B. Encode their posterior means and decode a straight line between the means. For every arm, report whether it reconstructs the correct endpoint molecules. On the original 128-pair frozen evaluation, all canonical endpoint pairs reconstructed correctly, and 66 midpoints were valid. Reordering B into A's frame before encoding made 126 midpoints valid, but only 38 pairs still decoded to both correct endpoint identities. Among the 30 pairs whose canonical map was already the identity, all 30 canonical midpoints were valid. The pairs requiring reordering were a different cohort, so the difference cannot measure a pure permutation effect. [Frozen paths and endpoint controls](../experiments/vae/interpolation.md#completed--3-november-2026-124024-cet)

**Blended labels.** The first training pilot used 400 updates from epoch 45 per arm, 32 real reconstruction examples per update, and a shared frozen prior anchor. Its treatment added two mapped pairs per update at nine interior fractions from 10% to 90%. It mixed the endpoint labels independently for each categorical atom, charge and bond target. The treatment used 18 additional latent decodes per update. All 128 evaluation pairs retained both correct endpoints. Mean divergence from the soft targets dropped from 0.02140 to 0.02002 versus the control, yet valid argmax midpoints fell from 68 to 64. The exact target marginals themselves produced only 42 valid argmax midpoints out of 128. Compatible choices of atoms and bonds can get separated when each label is mixed independently. On the same pairs, a follow-up adding chemistry loss at interpolated points recovered two midpoints to reach 66/128, versus 68/128 for the control. A finite repair gradient on a bad midpoint did not establish a general benefit from retraining the shared decoder. ## The recovery continuation

The user selected a **recovery attempt from the affected weights during epoch 47**. A matched fork from healthy epoch 46 would answer whether a loss prevents collapse. The current branch instead asks whether genuine PubChem and ChEMBL reconstruction gains can survive while the existing model relearns plausible random samples. This choice makes the existing partial checkpoint the student and the completed epoch-46 checkpoint a frozen reference.

For a logical batch, keep reconstruction and KL on 240 true training molecules. Draw 16 new standard-normal vectors from the saved independent prior generator. Decode the same vectors with the student and reference. The new prior objective is

```text
0.375 * structural bond repair
+ 1.0 * soft occupancy cross-entropy against frozen epoch 46
+ 1.0 * conditional chemistry cross-entropy on eligible epoch-46 graphs
```

- **Bond repair.** Search on the current student graph with the structural teacher. A successful witness supplies labels on the unordered occupied pairs. Average their conditional bond cross-entropy per graph, then average across all scheduled prior rows. Rows without a repaired target contribute zero to this particular term. The bond-only target removes explicit supervision on the student's own element, charge and occupancy choices.
- **Occupancy.** For every slot of every vector form `u = logsumexp(real_element_logits) - no_atom_logit`. Use binary cross-entropy with logits against the detached epoch-46 occupancy probability for that same vector and slot. The derivative with respect to `u` is the student's occupancy minus the reference's occupancy, before averaging. Thus a confidently occupied slot can still receive a strong corrective gradient if the reference predicts absence. Each vector can have a different target count. The parent need not give a chemically valid hard graph for this term to apply.
- **Reference chemistry.** Take the frozen reference's hard graph at the same vector. Check eligibility by chemical validity and the representational round trip. On accepted rows use element CE over the real-element classes on occupied slots, charge CE on those slots, and bond CE over unordered occupied pairs. The component weights are 1, 1 and 2. No-bond has class weight 0.1. Multiply the combined term by the fraction of eligible batch rows. Excluding the empty class from the element softmax removes its direct conditional-label gradient. The shared refinement parameters can still influence it indirectly.

The live forward/backward pass stays differentiable through the student logits. The detached search, chemistry checks and all frozen reference operations sit outside this path. This matters when porting the objective to Mosaic or rewriting it against other tensor types. Distinguish the reference atom/charge/bond term from the structural repair branch, whose bond normalization is a different reduction. Preserve the original resume checkpoint, AdamW state, train-row cursor, permutation and generators when asking about this run. Across 24 matched starts, guidance changed count for 15 starts versus 9 with random search. Random search evaluated 1,591 cheap directions versus 90 guided ones and obtained a lower mean fresh loss, 2.58340 versus 2.61254, at a similar native-call spend. The completed larger campaign logged 20,000 accepted updates across 6,498 trajectories, including 9,027 chemistry changes, 3,327 additions and 2,018 removals. Restarts create new baselines. These observations show that a VAE can change its own molecule size under this proposal and verification procedure. They do not establish superiority over inexpensive random search. ### Decisions the current evidence supports

**For a proposal about “more data,” specify its intended benefit.** The partial 60M run is positive evidence for reconstruction of held-out molecules from the two additional sources. It is negative evidence for direct Gaussian generation under the original 15:1 loss. The joint change in data and supervision prevents assigning either effect solely to corpus size. If a future agent needs a causal answer, compare the two losses from the same healthy checkpoint with matched examples, priors, update counts and optimizer state. Include reconstruction by source and atom count, including a deliberately sampled rare-size validation panel if claiming performance near 50 atoms.

**For a proposal about smoothness, define a useful edit.** A locally continuous relaxed graph can keep the same hard identity for many steps. A larger step can cross several identity boundaries and produce an invalid structure. Specify the starting distribution, radius, temperature, fixed-slot policy, success denominator and comparison direction. Measure how far it takes to reach a changed valid graph, whether the fresh score improves, and what happens on random directions at the same budget. The failed blended-interpolation experiment and more selective triplet study are the historical controls for any new interpolation loss.

**For a proposal about chemical validity, name the failure mode.** Empty graphs, disconnected graphs, impossible valence, representation round-trip failures, excessive sulfur, constant 50-atom graphs and repeated all-carbon identities require different diagnostics. A better local-valence score can coexist with more disconnected graphs. A repair search may produce a valid witness even when the raw graph remains bad. Quote the prevalence of each class, the rows on which a term applies and whether the signal comes from the student's graph, a search witness, a previous checkpoint or the actual training molecule.

**For design integration, put a price on each discovery.** Keep the parent identity, latent, scored mask, raw graph and fresh graph attached to every branch of an adaptive search. Charge all scorer calls, failed conformers and restarts to the appropriate arm. For variable-size runs, include additions, removals, realized changes that survive unmasked decoding, and fresh score differences within comparable size ranges. The technically stronger result is a larger number of diverse, valid, independently rescored improvements than a direct graph-edit or random baseline under the same controller. The existing evidence makes that a well-posed next comparison. It remains an open result. [Cross-codebase review and comparator design](project-review-2026-11-18.md) · [Representation comparison and alternatives](../experiments/vae/representation-search-review.md)

**If proposing an alternative to a VAE, identify the property it would buy.** A motif or junction-tree decoder could constrain fragment assembly but would need a way to expose useful derivatives or to enter the adaptive search as a discrete proposal. A parent-plus-edit model could learn coordinated substitutions, additions and deletions directly. A learned nonlinear prior would address unconditional initialization, with a separate test for whether it also improves movement near known founders. These are candidate experiments. The nearest ready comparison is already available through direct graph edits under the same controller.
